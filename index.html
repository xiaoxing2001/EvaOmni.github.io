Fewer shortcuts. A fairer test.

Expert annotation and human cross-validation meet text-only, audio-only, and visual-only screening to reduce shortcut-based success.

Human verified · Shortcut screened

Gemini3.7-Flash: 79.4% accuracy. Only 35.1% bottleneck grounded score. Even the strongest evaluated model leaves a substantial gap between answering correctly and grounding its answer.

LEVEL 1 · EVENT BINDINGQA #0002

What event is on screen when the narration says, "With the Weisswurst to be one of the city's best"?

  1. A worker is placing several white sausages into a display case filled with different sausage products.
  2. A man is cutting apart a connected string of white sausages on a work surface.
  3. A man is arranging several freshly prepared white sausages on a metal tray.
  4. A man is standing beside rows of white sausages hanging behind the counter.
  5. A man is transferring several white sausages from a metal container onto a serving tray.
  6. A man is lifting and showing several strings of sausages.
  7. A man is examining several freshly made white sausages while standing inside the shop.
  8. A man is holding up a long string of freshly made white sausages beside the preparation table.
ANNOTATED ANSWER

F. A man is lifting and showing several strings of sausages.

The evidence behind the answer Human annotated
